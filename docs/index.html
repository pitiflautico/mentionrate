How it works

  1. You tell us your product and two competitors. We write the questions a buyer in your category would ask.
  2. We ask each question several times, because assistants do not answer the same way twice, and store every answer.
  3. A fixed counting rule decides who was named and who was cited. No model grades the answers.

What we do not promise

  • We measure. Whether a given change gets you named more often is an open question in this field, so we test it: the success rule is written down before the change, and the report says so when nothing moved.
  • The numbers come from one assistant through its programming interface, which can differ from what a person sees in the app.
  • With a few dozen answers the margins are wide. Every report shows them.
